# Phase 3 — Task 4: Stand Up and Defend It

**Issued by:** Promise Ekeh, Head of Analytics
**Phase:** 3 — Visualisation & Dashboards
**Duration:** ~Week 3 of the phase · part-time
**Prerequisite:** [Task 3 — Executive Dashboard](task3_executive_dashboard.md).

---

## Context

A dashboard on a screen isn't finished until you can present it. In this task you prepare and deliver a short talk to a simulated panel (me, plus one or two analysts playing Eleanor and her CFO), then take questions. This is the skill that turns good analysis into influence.

Everything is in **pounds (£)**.

## Objective

Tell the NorthStar story in a 10-minute talk built around a single narrative arc, and handle 5 minutes of Q&A calmly.

## Working structure

```
tasks/phase3/
├── phase3_presentation.pptx        (or slides link)
└── phase3_presentation_notes.md    (narrative, Q&A prep, post-mortem)
```

## Requirements

1. **Narrative first.** In the speaker notes of slide 1, write the one-paragraph arc: *"NorthStar is broadly healthy, but London is overperforming and Wales is sliding; returns are climbing in Clothing. We recommend X, Y, Z."* Build slides to support that arc.
2. **Deck (8–12 slides):** title; context & objective; **one finding per slide** (sentence headline, one chart, one "so what"); recommendations (three, each tied to a finding); risks/caveats; Q&A. Keep messy charts as **backup slides**.
3. **Rehearse.** Time it — 10 minutes is hard; if you're over, cut a slide, don't talk faster. Record yourself once, watch it, note two improvements.
4. **Q&A prep** in `phase3_presentation_notes.md`: eight questions you'd fear ("How confident are you in the 12% lift?", "Is the data trustworthy?", "What would you do with more time?") with 1–3 sentence answers.
5. **Deliver.** Book the 15-minute slot with me.
6. **Post-mortem.** Afterwards, write a short reflection: what landed, what didn't, what you'd change.

**Milestone:** you can tell the NorthStar story, cold, to a room that hasn't seen your work.

## Deliverables

## How I'll assess it

The usual four dimensions, weighted to **communication**: a clear arc, one idea per slide, and calm, honest Q&A answers.
